How to create your app icons:

  1. Click the buttons below to download the icons
  2. Right-click on each canvas and "Save image as..."
  3. Save as icon-192.png and icon-512.png
  4. Place the PNG files in your project root folder

Or use an online tool: Upload icon.svg to realfavicongenerator.net or similar service.
